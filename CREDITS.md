# Credits (assets chosen for the game)
Every asset below is **CC0 1.0** (public domain), so **no attribution is legally required** and none needs a sign-up. We credit the creators anyway, and Poly Haven in particular asks that it is made clear its assets came from them. Only CC0 items are in this list: nothing CC-BY, so there are no mandatory attribution strings.

Files live in `../assets/` (see `../assets/LICENSES.md`); the wider shortlist is `../research/free-assets.md`.

## Suggested credits-screen text (copy into the game)
> Terrain textures, HDR skies and rocks: Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), CC0.
> Low-poly scenery and vehicles: Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0.
> Sound recordings: BigSoundBank.com (mainly Joseph SARDIN, also Denis Chardonnet, Pierre Sibanarco, Dorian Clair, gamin) and OpenGameArt.org contributors rubberduck, domasx2, pauliuw, TinyWorlds, EZduzziteh and Yaroslav_Novikov, all CC0.
> Libraries: three.js (MIT), Rapier (Apache-2.0), PeerJS (MIT), qrcode-generator (MIT).
