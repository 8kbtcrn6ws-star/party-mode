Source files (assets/models/kenney-nature-kit/*.glb) were uniformly rescaled to real-world metres, re-centred so the
base sits at y=0 (x/z centred), and in a few cases (snowy pines, sandstone rocks, dry scrub) had a flat material colour
changed. No Poly Haven or Quaternius assets are used. Mapping of prototype -> source file: props_manifest.json.
